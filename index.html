FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

A fog bubble machine is a device that produces fog-filled bubbles. It is more than just a fog machine combined with a bubble maker — it injects fog into each bubble before the bubble is fully formed and blown out.

雾泡机是一种能制造充满烟雾的泡泡的设备。它不只是烟雾机与泡泡机的简单组合——它会在每个泡泡完全成型并被吹出之前，将烟雾注入其中。

The device uses regular bubble solution and fog fluid at the same time. The two liquids are poured into separate tanks. The machine then takes 3–5 minutes to heat up until it can convert the fog fluid into smoke.

设备同时使用普通泡泡液和烟雾油。两种液体分别倒入独立的液箱。机器需要 3–5 分钟预热，才能将烟雾油转化为烟雾。

Fog fluid normally contains deionized water, propylene glycol and glycerol. Deionized water is non-toxic. Propylene glycol is low-toxicity and safe when used as regulated. Glycerol has extremely low toxicity and is a normal metabolic intermediate, considered safe under normal use. Avoid formulations containing ethylene glycol, which is more hazardous.

烟雾油通常含有去离子水、丙二醇和甘油。去离子水无毒；丙二醇属低毒，按规定使用安全；甘油毒性极低，是人体正常代谢中间物，正常使用安全。应避免含乙二醇的配方，其危害相对更大。

A fog bubble machine not only combines both functions, but precisely traps fog inside the bubbles. When the bubbles pop, the fog is released and drifts through the air, creating an interesting and magical effect with strong visual impact.

雾泡机不仅集两种功能于一身，更将烟雾精准锁在泡泡内部。泡泡破裂时，烟雾释放并在空中飘散，营造出极具视觉冲击的奇幻效果。

Any fog bubble machine with AC power must be used by adults. A battery-powered fog bubble machine can be used by children aged 3 and above under adult supervision.

任何使用交流电源（插电）的雾泡机必须由成人操作。电池供电的雾泡机可在成人监护下供 3 岁及以上儿童使用。

They can be used in many settings: outdoor weddings, Halloween celebrations, family parties, camping, shopping mall events, night clubs, disco halls, small stages, parks, kindergartens, pop-up shops, convenience store vans, or any place that needs fun.

可用于众多场合：户外婚礼、万圣节庆祝、家庭聚会、露营、商场活动、夜店、迪厅、小型舞台、公园、幼儿园、快闪店、便利店外摆车，或任何需要欢乐氛围的场所。
